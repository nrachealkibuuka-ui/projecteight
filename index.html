Welcome to Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026

The Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026 is a premier international event organised by the Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries (MAAIF). The expo brings together coffee farmers, processors, exporters, investors and industry professionals to celebrate Uganda's rich coffee heritage.

Over three exciting days, participants will explore modern coffee production technologies, sustainable farming practices, global market opportunities and innovative business solutions. The event will also create opportunities for networking, knowledge sharing and international trade partnerships.
